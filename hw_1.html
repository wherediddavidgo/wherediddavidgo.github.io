Outliers to be identified as points beyond 1.5x the IQR of the log-transformed data.

logQ = np.log10(meanq)

median = np.median(logQ)
q1 = np.percentile(logQ, 25)
q3 = np.percentile(logQ, 75)
iqr = q3 - q1

upper_whisker = q3 + iqr
lower_whisker = q1 - iqr

upper_outliers = logQ.loc[(logQ > upper_whisker)]
lower_outliers = logQ.loc[(logQ < lower_whisker)]

The relationship between Q and P is approximately linear or very slightly concave up. It appears to depart from the regression line at the low end because at Q = 0, P =~ 1.5, and both variables are bounded by 0. It departs from the regression line at the high end for a less easily discernible reason.
