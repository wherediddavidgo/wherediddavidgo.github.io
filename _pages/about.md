I am a 2nd year PhD Student in the [Global Rivers Group](https://globalriversgroup.com) in the Department of Geosciences at Virginia Tech. River networks are hotspots of greenhouse gas exchange, and a key parameter used in estimating greenhouse gas flux is surface area.

I use satellite remote sensing and fieldwork to understand how river networks expand and contract in length and width from the headwater to the continental scale to constrain river surface area for better carbon accounting and explore connections between hydrology, geomorphology, and biogeochemistry.
